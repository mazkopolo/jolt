Privacy Policy

Jolt — interval timer for functional fitness
Last updated: 27 July 2026

Jolt does not collect any data about you.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. Jolt contains no tracking software of any kind, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us — because there is no server to send it to.

What Jolt stores, and where

Everything Jolt saves stays on your device, inside the app's own private storage:

  • Your workouts — names, rounds, reps and timings.
  • Your workout history — the date, duration and round count of sessions you finish.
  • Your settings — chosen coach voice, accent colour, cue preferences.

None of it leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it. If you use iCloud device backups, this data may be included in your own backup, which is controlled by your Apple Account settings rather than by Jolt.

Sharing a workout

When you share a workout, the entire workout is encoded inside the link itself. No copy is uploaded anywhere, and we never see it. Whoever you send it to can open it in Jolt, and nothing about that exchange reaches us.

Purchases

Jolt Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles the entire transaction. Jolt never receives or stores your name, payment details or Apple Account information — it only asks the operating system whether a valid purchase exists on the device, and that check works offline.

Health and fitness data

Jolt does not read from or write to Apple Health, and does not request access to it. It also does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or your location.

Children

Jolt is a workout timer suitable for general audiences. Because it collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.
